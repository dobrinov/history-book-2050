# Second edition · research plan, outline, KPI list

## Peers
BG vs RO, HR, RS, SK, PL, EU27. RO is the closest twin (same accession date, similar
starting point) and the primary counterfactual. RS = the non-EU path. SK/PL = the
"fast reformers". HR = late EU entrant, euro 2023.

## KPI list (by dimension)
1. Everyday life — AIC per capita PPS (prc_ppp_ind A01), median equivalised income PPS
   (ilc_di03), HICP (prc_hicp_aind), unemployment (une_rt_a; WB SL.UEM.TOTL.ZS pre-2000),
   employment 20-64 (lfsi_emp_a), severe material deprivation (ilc_mddd11), AROPE,
   life expectancy (demo_mlexpec; WB SP.DYN.LE00.IN), infant mortality, housing
   overcrowding (ilc_lvho05a), inability to keep home warm (ilc_mdes01), 65+ AROPE.
2. Economic foundations — GDP per capita PPS (prc_ppp_ind GDP), GDP PPP constant (WB
   NY.GDP.PCAP.PP.KD for peer index 1990=100), labour productivity (nama_10_lp_ulc),
   gov debt (gov_10dd_edpt1), FDI inflows % GDP (WB BX.KLT.DINV.WD.GD.ZS), energy import
   dependency (nrg_ind_id), EU net receipts (Commission operating budget balance).
3. Geopolitical — event-based: NATO 2004, EU 2007, Schengen 2024/25, euro 2026; gas
   share from Russia (pre/post 2022); share of exports to EU (WB/Eurostat); no single
   index — qualitative judgement vs. realistic options.
4. Institutions — TI CPI, RSF press freedom rank, WGI rule of law (WB GE/RL, perception),
   government durability (cabinets per decade), voter turnout (CIK / IDEA).
5. Society & demography — population index 1990=100 (WB SP.POP.TOTL) vs peers,
   emigration, fertility, PISA reading (OECD), early school leavers, regional GDP per
   capita PPS Sofia (BG411) vs Severozapaden (BG31) (nama_10r_2gdp), Roma/minority
   (FRA surveys — qualitative, cited).

## Attribution rules
- A government is measured from its first full year to the last full year in office,
  plus a one-year lag for everyday-life KPIs.
- "Relative" = BG change minus the average change of RO/HR/PL/SK over the same window.
- Two marks per dimension: "in term" and "legacy (from 2050)". Scale: −2..+2, with
  "?" for unclear. Reader chooses weights with sliders.

## Chapter outline (turning points)
0. Prologue 1984–89: Revival Process, the "Great Excursion", debt and shortages.
I. 1989–91 The negotiated exit (Round Table, 1991 Constitution, price liberalisation).
II. 1991–96 Who got the state? (land restitution, credit millionaires, wrestlers,
    the privatisation model that did not happen).
III. 1996–97 The winter that changed everything (bank collapse, hyperinflation,
    January 1997 protests).
IV. 1997–2001 The currency board (decision + alternatives; mass privatisation; NHIF).
V. 2001–05 Anchoring west (NATO, EU treaty, Kozloduy closures as the price).
VI. 2005–09 Boom, flat tax, frozen funds, the gas crisis and Russian energy projects
    (Belene, South Stream, Burgas–Alexandroupolis).
VII. 2009–13 Austerity and the energy bills.
VIII. 2013–14 #ДАНСwithme and KTB.
IX. 2014–21 Growth without rule of law (prosecutor's office, media, 2020 protests).
X. 2020–21 The pandemic: excess mortality and trust.
XI. 2021–25 Seven elections, the Gazprom cut-off, Schengen, the euro decision.
XII. 2026 Euro and Radev.
